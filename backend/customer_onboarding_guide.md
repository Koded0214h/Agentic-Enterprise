# Customer Onboarding Guide: Freelance Invoice & Payment Tracker

This guide ensures new paying customers successfully set up and utilize our product, maximizing their initial experience and driving long-term retention.

## 1. Welcome & Initial Setup (First 24 Hours)

*   **Welcome Email**: Send an automated welcome email immediately after signup.
    *   **Subject**: Welcome to [Product Name]! Let's Get You Started.
    *   **Content**: Thank them, provide login link, link to this onboarding guide, and a direct link to schedule a 15-min personalized onboarding call (optional but recommended for early adopters).
    *   **Key Action**: Encourage them to complete their profile.
*   **Profile Completion**: Prompt users to complete their profile information.
    *   **Required**: Company Name (or Freelancer Name), Address, Contact Email, Payment Details (for receiving payments).
    *   **Optional**: Logo upload, branding colors.
*   **Connect Payment Gateway**: Guide users through connecting their preferred payment gateway (e.g., Stripe, PayPal).
    *   Provide clear, step-by-step instructions with screenshots or a short video tutorial.
*   **First Client Setup**: Encourage them to add their first client.
    *   **Action**: Navigate to "Clients" section -> "Add New Client."
    *   **Guidance**: Explain required fields (Client Name, Email) and optional fields.

## 2. Creating the First Invoice (Day 1-3)

*   **Interactive Walkthrough/Tooltip**: Implement an in-app walkthrough or tooltips to guide users through creating their first invoice.
*   **Step-by-Step Instructions**:
    1.  Navigate to "Invoices" -> "Create New Invoice."
    2.  Select a Client (from previously added or add new).
    3.  Add Invoice Items (Service Description, Quantity, Rate).
    4.  Set Due Date and Payment Terms.
    5.  Preview Invoice.
    6.  Send Invoice (via email directly from the platform).
*   **Template Customization**: Highlight how to customize invoice templates with their logo and branding.
*   **Resource**: Link to a "How to Create Your First Invoice" video tutorial.

## 3. Tracking Payments & Managing Contracts (Day 3-7)

*   **Payment Tracking**: Explain how to mark invoices as paid and track payment statuses.
    *   **Action**: Go to "Invoices" -> Select an invoice -> "Mark as Paid" or view payment history.
    *   **Benefit**: Emphasize the dashboard overview for quick financial insights.
*   **Contract Management**: Guide users on uploading and associating contracts with clients/projects.
    *   **Action**: Navigate to "Clients" or "Projects" -> "Add Contract."
    *   **Benefit**: Stress the importance of having all client documentation in one place.
*   **Reminders & Automation**: Show users how to set up automated payment reminders for overdue invoices.
    *   **Action**: Go to "Settings" -> "Automated Reminders."
    *   **Benefit**: "Get paid on time, every time, without manual follow-ups."

## 4. Advanced Features & Ongoing Support (Week 2+)

*   **Reporting**: Introduce the reporting features for financial overview (e.g., income reports, outstanding invoices).
*   **Integrations**: Highlight any available integrations (e.g., calendar, project management tools).
*   **Feedback Loop**: Encourage users to provide feedback through an in-app widget or dedicated email.
*   **Support Channels**: Clearly list all support channels:
    *   **Knowledge Base/FAQ**: Link to comprehensive documentation.
    *   **Email Support**: Dedicated support email address.
    *   **Live Chat**: If available.
    *   **Community Forum**: If applicable.
*   **Regular Communication**: Send periodic emails with tips, new feature announcements, and best practices.

## 5. Success Metrics for Onboarding

*   **Activation Rate**: Percentage of users who complete core setup (profile, first client, first invoice) within 7 days.
*   **Time to First Invoice Sent**: Average time from signup to sending the first invoice.
*   **Feature Adoption**: Usage of key features like payment tracking, contract management, and automated reminders.
*   **Churn Rate (Early)**: Monitor churn within the first 30-60 days to identify onboarding friction points.
*   **NPS Score**: Gather Net Promoter Score after 30 days to gauge overall satisfaction.
