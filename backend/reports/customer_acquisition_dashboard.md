# Customer Acquisition Dashboard Template

## Goal
To provide a clear, real-time overview of our progress towards acquiring 100 paying users, identifying trends, and informing strategic adjustments.

## Key Metrics to Track

### 1. Top-Level Goal
*   **Metric**: **Number of Paying Users**
    *   *Description*: Total count of active, subscribed users.
    *   *Target*: 100
    *   *Visualization*: Large number display, progress bar towards 100.

### 2. Funnel Metrics
*   **Metric**: **Website Visitors**
    *   *Description*: Total unique visitors to our landing pages and product site.
    *   *Visualization*: Line chart (daily/weekly/monthly).
*   **Metric**: **Lead Magnet Downloads / Email Sign-ups**
    *   *Description*: Number of users who downloaded a lead magnet or signed up for our newsletter.
    *   *Visualization*: Bar chart (daily/weekly).
*   **Metric**: **Free Trial Sign-ups**
    *   *Description*: Number of new users who started a free trial.
    *   *Visualization*: Line chart (daily/weekly).
*   **Metric**: **Free Trial to Paid Conversion Rate**
    *   *Description*: Percentage of free trial users who convert to a paid subscription.
    *   *Formula*: (Paying Users / Free Trial Sign-ups) * 100
    *   *Visualization*: Gauge or percentage display.

### 3. Channel Performance
*   **Metric**: **Traffic by Source**
    *   *Description*: Breakdown of website visitors by acquisition channel (e.g., Organic Search, Paid Social, Referral, Direct, Email).
    *   *Visualization*: Pie chart or stacked bar chart.
*   **Metric**: **Free Trial Sign-ups by Channel**
    *   *Description*: Number of free trial sign-ups attributed to each marketing channel.
    *   *Visualization*: Bar chart.
*   **Metric**: **Paying Users by Channel**
    *   *Description*: Number of paying users attributed to each marketing channel.
    *   *Visualization*: Bar chart.

### 4. Cost & Efficiency Metrics
*   **Metric**: **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**
    *   *Description*: Total marketing and sales spend divided by the number of new paying customers acquired.
    *   *Formula*: (Total Marketing & Sales Spend) / (New Paying Users)
    *   *Visualization*: Single number display.
*   **Metric**: **Cost Per Lead (CPL)**
    *   *Description*: Total marketing spend divided by the number of leads generated (e.g., lead magnet downloads).
    *   *Visualization*: Single number display.
*   **Metric**: **Cost Per Free Trial Sign-up**
    *   *Description*: Total marketing spend divided by the number of free trial sign-ups.
    *   *Visualization*: Single number display.

### 5. Engagement & Onboarding Metrics (from `onboarding_optimization_plan.md`)
*   **Metric**: **Onboarding Checklist Completion Rate**
    *   *Description*: Percentage of users who complete the in-app onboarding checklist.
    *   *Visualization*: Percentage display.
*   **Metric**: **Time to First Invoice Sent**
    *   *Description*: Average time taken for a new user to send their first invoice.
    *   *Visualization*: Average time display.
*   **Metric**: **Feature Adoption Rate (e.g., Contracts)**
    *   *Description*: Percentage of active users who utilize key features like contract generation.
    *   *Visualization*: Percentage display.

## Reporting Frequency
*   **Daily**: Quick check on top-level metrics (paying users, trial sign-ups).
*   **Weekly**: Detailed review of all metrics, identify anomalies, discuss immediate tactical adjustments.
*   **Monthly**: Comprehensive analysis of trends, channel performance, CAC, and strategic planning for the next month.
