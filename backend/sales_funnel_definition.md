# Sales Funnel Definition: Reaching 100 Paying Customers

This document outlines the stages of our sales process, key conversion metrics, and departmental responsibilities to guide prospects from initial awareness to becoming active, paying customers.

## 1. Sales Funnel Stages

### Stage 1: Awareness (Marketing)
*   **Description**: Potential customers become aware of our product through various marketing efforts.
*   **Activities**:
    *   Content marketing (blog posts, guides)
    *   Social media presence
    *   Community engagement
    *   Direct outreach (initial contact)
*   **Key Metrics**:
    *   Website traffic
    *   Social media impressions/engagement
    *   Blog post views
    *   LinkedIn connection acceptance rate
    *   Email open rates
*   **Responsible Department**: Marketing

### Stage 2: Interest (Marketing/Sales)
*   **Description**: Prospects show initial interest in our solution.
*   **Activities**:
    *   Engagement with content (downloads, shares)
    *   Clicking on CTAs
    *   Responding to direct outreach
    *   Visiting product pages
    *   Signing up for newsletters/webinars
*   **Key Metrics**:
    *   Click-through rates (CTRs)
    *   Lead magnet downloads
    *   Webinar registrations
    *   Replies to outreach messages
*   **Responsible Department**: Marketing, Sales (for qualified leads)

### Stage 3: Consideration (Sales)
*   **Description**: Prospects actively evaluate our product as a potential solution.
*   **Activities**:
    *   Free trial sign-ups
    *   Product demos/webinars
    *   Direct conversations with sales/support
    *   Reviewing pricing plans
*   **Key Metrics**:
    *   Free trial conversion rate (from interest to trial)
    *   Demo attendance rate
    *   Engagement with product features during trial
    *   Support ticket inquiries
*   **Responsible Department**: Sales, Product Support

### Stage 4: Decision (Sales)
*   **Description**: Prospects decide to purchase our product.
*   **Activities**:
    *   Subscription to a paid plan
    *   Payment processing
    *   Contract signing (if applicable)
*   **Key Metrics**:
    *   Paid conversion rate (from trial to paid)
    *   Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
    *   Churn rate (post-conversion)
*   **Responsible Department**: Sales, Billing

### Stage 5: Retention/Advocacy (Customer Success)
*   **Description**: Existing customers continue to use and advocate for our product.
*   **Activities**:
    *   Onboarding support
    *   Ongoing customer support
    *   Feature updates and communication
    *   Gathering feedback
    *   Referral program participation
*   **Key Metrics**:
    *   Customer retention rate
    *   Net Promoter Score (NPS)
    *   Feature adoption rate
    *   Referral sign-ups
*   **Responsible Department**: Customer Success, Product

## 2. Key Conversion Metrics & Goals

To reach 100 paying customers, we will set conversion goals for each stage. (These are illustrative and will be refined with data).

*   **Awareness to Interest**: 10% (e.g., 1000 website visitors -> 100 interested leads)
*   **Interest to Consideration (Free Trial)**: 20% (e.g., 100 interested leads -> 20 free trials)
*   **Consideration (Free Trial) to Decision (Paid)**: 25% (e.g., 20 free trials -> 5 paying customers)

**Target Calculation**: To get 100 paying customers with a 25% trial-to-paid conversion, we need 400 free trials. To get 400 free trials with a 20% interest-to-trial conversion, we need 2000 interested leads. To get 2000 interested leads with a 10% awareness-to-interest conversion, we need 20,000 awareness touchpoints.

## 3. Departmental Responsibilities

*   **Marketing**: Top-of-funnel activities, lead generation, content creation, brand awareness.
*   **Sales**: Lead qualification, product demonstrations, closing deals, managing prospect relationships.
*   **Product Support**: Assisting trial users, resolving technical issues, providing feature guidance.
*   **Customer Success**: Onboarding new customers, ongoing support, fostering long-term relationships, gathering feedback.
*   **Product**: Enhancing features based on feedback, ensuring product-market fit, improving user experience.
