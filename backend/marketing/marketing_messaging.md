# Marketing Messaging: Freelance Invoice & Payment Tracker MVP

Based on the 'Growing Freelance Designer' persona, our messaging focuses on solving their core pain points: administrative overhead, cash flow uncertainty, and the desire for professionalism and growth.

## Core Value Proposition

**"Spend less time on invoices, more time on design. Get paid faster, look more professional."**

## Key Message Pillars

1.  **Time-Saving Automation:** Highlight how the tool frees up designers from tedious admin.
2.  **Improved Cash Flow:** Emphasize faster payments and better financial visibility.
3.  **Professional Image:** Focus on polished, customizable invoices that impress clients.
4.  **Simplicity & Focus:** Stress the ease of use and how it's built specifically for designers.
